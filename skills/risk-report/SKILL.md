# Risk Report

Use this skill to act as the quality gate and final report integrator.

Output:
- Core risks
- Counter-thesis checks
- Risk triggers
- Evidence citation review
- Final report-ready summary

Rules:
- Do not add unsupported facts.
- Do not hide data gaps.
- Separate evidence-backed risks from assumptions.
