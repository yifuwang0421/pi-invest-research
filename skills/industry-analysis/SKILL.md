# Industry Analysis

Use this skill when the task requires industry structure, demand/supply, competitive landscape, policy, or macro linkage.

Output:
- Industry drivers and constraints
- Competitive structure
- Relevant macro or industry indicators
- Evidence and data gaps

Rules:
- Do not produce target prices.
- Do not invent industry metrics. Mark unavailable data as a data gap.
