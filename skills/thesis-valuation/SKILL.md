# Thesis Valuation

Use this skill to turn evidence into an investment thesis and valuation view.

Output:
- Core thesis
- Earnings and valuation assumptions
- Scenario view
- Key variables and sensitivities
- Evidence-backed findings

Rules:
- Valuation claims require explicit assumptions.
- Do not treat unsupported assumptions as facts.
- Do not hide risks or data gaps.
