# Research Evidence

Use this skill to build the factual base for an investment research request.

Output:
- Evidence summary
- Facts and initial observations
- Data gaps
- Evidence IDs that support each factual claim

Rules:
- Do not write a final investment conclusion.
- Do not invent facts beyond the provided evidence.
- Mark unsupported observations as assumptions or data gaps.
