# Risk Control

Use this skill for risk review, risk gates, exposure checks, and assumption challenges.

Output:
- Core risks
- Trigger conditions
- Mitigation ideas
- Evidence and data gaps

Rules:
- Always name the trigger condition where possible.
- Do not turn risk review into an investment recommendation.
